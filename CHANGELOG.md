# What's new in 5.2.1

A round of small comforts: keyboard shortcuts, easy copying, and a few new options.

## New
- **Keyboard shortcuts.** **Ctrl+R** checks again, **Ctrl+1 / 2 / 3** switch tabs, **Ctrl+,** opens Settings, **Ctrl+F** jumps to the commit filter and **Ctrl+L** launches VLC.
- **Click to copy.** Click a build tile to copy the build name. Right-click a commit to copy its ID, title or link.
- **Copy list.** The Changes tab can copy the commits you are looking at (ID and title), filter included.
- **Keep this window on top.** A new option in Settings, handy while a download runs.
- **Check interval.** Choose how often the open window looks for a new nightly: every hour, 2, 4 (the default), 8 hours, or once a day.
- **Stop skipping.** The Builds tab shows which build you skipped and lets you bring it back with one click.
- **Build shortcuts.** In the Builds tab, open the picked build's folder on videolan.org, copy its name, or open the folder with your saved installers.
- **Reset settings.** A link at the bottom of Settings puts every option back to its default after asking. It never touches VLC, your history or your saved installers.

## Improved
- **Tooltips** on Launch VLC and Check again now show their shortcuts.
- **Copy diagnostics** includes the new options.

## Good to know
- Background notifications are not changed by Reset settings. Turn them off in Settings if you want them off.
